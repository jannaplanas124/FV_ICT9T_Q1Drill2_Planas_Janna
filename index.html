OB Montessori Center




At Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center, every child learns through Dr. Maria Montessori's hollistic approach. OBMC is a progressive pre-k to 12 co-educational school in which we blend tradition and innovation in preparation for the child's life.

Our mission is to prepare students in becoming the future leaders of the society. Explore our programs and discover the vibrant community of OBMC.
